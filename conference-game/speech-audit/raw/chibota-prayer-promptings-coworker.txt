As we seek the serious perspective,
he will often guide us to those who need to be seen.
Years ago, I prayed to know how I could help a co-worker
who seemed isolated as I acted on simple promptings
to include him, our friendship room.
I learned that when we asked Heavenly Father
to help us see others,
he guides us to those who feel unseen.
You too can make a difference in someone's life.
