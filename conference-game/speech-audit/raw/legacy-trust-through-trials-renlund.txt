Yet God invites us to trust Him, and His promises.
During His mortal ministry, the Savior told His disciples, "These things I've spoken
to you, that in me, ye might have peace.
In the world ye shall have tribulation, but be of good cheer; I've overcome the world."
Jesus overcame the world.
We don't have to do so ourselves.
When our faith is centered on Jesus Christ, He promises spiritual peace no matter our circumstances.
