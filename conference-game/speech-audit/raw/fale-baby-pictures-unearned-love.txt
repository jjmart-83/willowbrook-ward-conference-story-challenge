impression to do what the teacher was doing. So in the text, I asked my wife to find a
baby picture of each of our children. When I got home, we sat down and showed my kids
their pictures. I told them that there are no words to fully express the love we felt
But when they were born, including the day each picture was taken, I then asked, "As
of the date of this picture, what had you done to earn our love?"
They lowered their eyes and said, "Nothing."
I asked them to look up and said, "You are right.
You did nothing to earn our love.
By that same token, there is nothing you can ever do that will unearn or remove that love
from you.
You did not earn it, you cannot lose it, it is yours forever.
I continued, we need you to always remember that what we feel is but a small fraction
of what our Heavenly Father feels for you.
You are His child and He loves you.
He loved you before you were born.
He loves you today and He will love you tomorrow.
He wants you to prepare to be like Him.
You must never forget this.
We hope this understanding gives you courage to do your best to trust Him and to live up
to your potential.
We also hope it gives you the strength and the humility to come home when you need to.
Because no matter what has happened, you can always come home just as you can always turn
to your Father in heaven.
There were tears in tender hearts as the Holy Spirit bore witness that this was true.
I referenced my children, but for you it might be a special love.
