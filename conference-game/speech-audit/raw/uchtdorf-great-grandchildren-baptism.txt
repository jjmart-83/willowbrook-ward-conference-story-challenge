It is such a joy to be with you today.
Recently, Sister Octa and I attended the baptism of two of our great-grandchildren.
We rejoiced with them as they chose to follow Jesus Christ and made sacred covenants to
take upon themselves the Savior's name, keep God's commandments and serve Him.
By entering the waters of baptism, they literally followed not only the Savior's example,
But also the example of siblings, cousins, parents,
and many trusted friends.
My heart fills with gratitude when I think back.
