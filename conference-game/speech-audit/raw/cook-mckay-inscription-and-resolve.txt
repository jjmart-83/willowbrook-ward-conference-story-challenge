On the grounds of one building, there is a replica of a message that significantly influenced
President David O. McKay when he was a young missionary in Scotland.
As a new missionary, Elder McKay passed a building where the stone above the doorway bore this
carved inscription, what er thou art act well thy part. This message, what er thou art
act well thy part, was so important and had such an impact on Elder McKay that he used
it for inspiration as a missionary and for the rest of his life. He determined that whatever
responsibility he had, he would do his very best. When I was young President
McKay was the prophet and his teachings had a lasting influence on me. I especially
loved this message and have often referred to it. It was clear that one of the
President of President McKay's purposes in mentioning this incident was to teach
that some conduct, which is not inherently evil, can keep us from engaging in
spiritual matters that will bless us now and throughout eternity. In our day, the
