When the Holy Spirit of promise witnesses and confirms the efficacy of ordinances and covenants,
they become binding in time and eternity.
In his sacred role as Holy Spirit of promise, the Holy Ghost witnesses to the Father
that the saving ordinances have been performed properly and that the covenants associated with them have been kept.
As we honor our covenants, the Holy Spirit of promise seals our relationships with efficacy, virtue, and force.
