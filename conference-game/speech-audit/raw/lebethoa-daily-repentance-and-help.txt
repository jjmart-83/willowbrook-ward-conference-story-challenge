Third, practice repentance daily.
Repentance means a change of mind and attaining of the heart to God.
Let us ponder about the changes that we need to make in our lives,
and these may include how we use our time, the things we watch or listen to,
and the language and tone we use as we communicate with others.
Perhaps we can begin by asking ourselves what is it that we need to change in our lives.
It is important to remember that we do not need to go through this alone.
The Savior is always ready to help because He sees the end from the beginning and understands
our weaknesses perfectly.
He is always ready to Saka His people according to their infirmities.
