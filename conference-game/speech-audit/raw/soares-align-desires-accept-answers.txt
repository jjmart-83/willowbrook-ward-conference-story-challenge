Adopting this holy pattern of fasting is more than a physical test of restraint or
a petition for specific blessings.
It is an elevating expression of faith and trust in the Lord, through which our hearts
can be filled with rejoicing as we commune with Him more deeply.
It is a way to align our desires with His purposes and our expectations with His wisdom.
Even when answers do not come in the way we expect, fasting and prayer helps us develop
greater confidence in the Lord, in our confidence with Him, and His plan for our lives, as well
as a deeper willingness to accept His will.
