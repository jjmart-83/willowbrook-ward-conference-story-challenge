As a child, I learned that greetings in my cultural communicate love, concern, respect,
and belonging.
My daughter recently reminded me of the significance of the Southern African greeting, "Saworna,"
which means "I see you."
This greeting is much more than a simple hello.
It is an acknowledgement of another person's worth and humanity.
It communicates, "I recognize you, I respect you, you matter."
The Savior Jesus Christ perfectly exemplifies.
