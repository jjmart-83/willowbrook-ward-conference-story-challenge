Maybe it's because I grew up hearing things like,
"If you keep the commandments, you'll be blessed.
Pay your tithing and the windows of heaven will open.
Follow the word of wisdom and you'll have health and protection."
True promises, right?
So the gospel felt like a big umbrella I could hold over my head
to keep the rain from falling on me.
But I have learned from experience
that we're all going to get rained on.
And sometimes we're going to get pelted.
The gospel isn't the umbrella.
It's the solid rock we're standing on during the storm.
that sure foundation were on if men build, they cannot fall.
When an angel asked Nephi, "Knowest thou the condescension of God?"
