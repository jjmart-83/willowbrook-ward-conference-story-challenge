Sometimes, we expect the Lord's places to come the way we would wish, rather than in
his own way and timing.
After a sacrament meeting, a faithful sister shared that her testimony of the love-tiding
had begun to weaken.
She explained that although she had always paid a full tie, she had never received the
job promotion she had hoped for.
or even the salary increase she deserved.
The council I gave her is the same council
that I share with you today.
In his address, the windows of heaven
elder David A. Bernard tapped.
As we leave the love tiding, we often receive significant
but subtle places that are not always what we expect,
and easily can be overlooked.
Elder Bernard's words prompted me to make a list
of the blessings I have received
through faithfully paying my tidings.
The Holy Ghost helped me recognize many blessings.
I have enjoyed studying employment for many years.
My wife, my children, and I have been blessed
with good health.
Our home and temporal possessions have been preserved
even during difficult times.
The Lord has blessed me with the ability
to manage my time and resources so I could obtain an education.
I have developed the gift of gratitude
by learning to appreciate the Lord's hand
in each of his tender mercies.
I have learned to recognize the influence of the Holy Ghost
when making important decisions in my life.
these blessings, both temporal and spiritual,
and many others are evidence that the Lord has indeed
opened the windows of heaven and pulled out
His promised blessings in abundance.
I invited that sister to write her own list
of the significant yet subtle places
the Lord had given her blessings that are often
easy to overlook.
Later, I heard another faithful sister share in a sacrament.
