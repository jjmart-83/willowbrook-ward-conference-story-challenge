Think for a moment about your favorite fruits.
Are they pineapples, bananas, and grapes, or mangoes, strawberries, and coconuts?
We can hold the fruit, feel it, and look at it.
We might smell it and guess how sweet it is.
But once we have tasted it, we know how much we like it.
truth is the same. We learn them at home, at primary and through the scriptures. They
become sweet to us as we live them, bringing us happiness and joy. Let me share three fruits
of the gospel.
