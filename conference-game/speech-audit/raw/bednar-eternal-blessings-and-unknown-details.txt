Now, you may ask, how can this be the case if a temple marriage does not endure?
The revealed doctrine of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints does not address
all details of and nuances about such cases.
I do not know and cannot explain precisely how and when promised blessings are received,
But I do know promised blessings will be received.
No faithful disciple will ultimately be deprived of any eternal blessing God has promised because
of another individual's unrighteous exercise of moral agency.
I conclude with a reassuring testimony of King Benjamin.
