I failed to see this with a friend who had at one time been spiritually faithful, but
was now attacking the church.
My frustration came not just because of his bitterness, but because I knew he knew better.
Unfortunately, rather than recognize the tension of the moment, or that he might be hurting
for reasons I could not see, I shot back an angry retort. I may have won the debate in
the moment, but I lost the spirit, and I failed to help my struggling friend. That day I stood
for truth, but I did it without love and without charity. A second way to stand for
truth with love.
