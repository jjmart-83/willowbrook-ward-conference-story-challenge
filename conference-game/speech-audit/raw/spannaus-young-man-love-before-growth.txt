Once a young man came running kneeling before Jesus Christ and asking him, "What
can I do to gain eternal life?" The young man said he had always obey the
commandments. Would Jesus know what else these young men could do? Of course, but
before he tells him look at what happened. In Mark 1021 we read, "Jesus, beholding
him, loved him. Can you imagine those moments when Jesus looks at this man, a
look that conveys only love, love of the highest quality? Only after transmitting
that love did he tell him what he could do to improve. Our Father and our Savior
know you and have the same perfect love for you.
Part of the love and mercy is giving us many opportunities and being patient as we learn
and work on our list of aspirations.
Dear young women, I testify to you.
