The Savior's invitation is not merely for us to add His name to our name like a
badge of religious affiliation. The Savior, why? Some draw near to me with
their lips, but their hearts are far from me. His invitation for us is to take upon
us His name. It is an invitation to become more like Him through
coven and keeping the resurrected Lord ask, have they not read the scriptures which say
ye mistake upon you the name of Christ, which is my name? How do we, as coven and children
of God, truly take upon ourselves the name of Jesus Christ? How does that manifest in
our daily lives? I love the simple, yet profound answer taught by the prophet Moroni. He
recorded that none were baptized, saved, they took upon them the name of Christ, having
a determination to serve Him to the end.
Taking upon us, His name requires a lifelong determination to serve Him, and King Benjamin
provided a divine key.
When ye are in the service of your fellow beings, ye are only in the service of your
God.
