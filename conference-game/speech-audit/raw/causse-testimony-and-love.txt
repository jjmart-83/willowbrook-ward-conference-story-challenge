Our testimony is not a talent to be hidden, it is a gift to be shared, with grateful hearts
and in the spirit of humidity and love.
Testimony and love are deeply connected gifts that echo and strengthen one another.
To say "I know" is a tender expression of love toward our neighbor, because we love others
we naturally desire to share with them the most precious gift we have received, our
witness of Jesus Christ and His gospel.
In turn, saying, "I love you," with a pure and sincere heart is a way of bearing testimony
of Jesus Christ.
Whenever we express Christlike love to those around us, we bear witness of Him, the source
of all perfect love.
Brothers and sisters, I hope you will fill your lives.
