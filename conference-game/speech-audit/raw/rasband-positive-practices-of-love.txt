Love is not stepping back or sanctioning others decisions and actions.
Love is being interested in their lives, their successes, and their challenges.
Love is being there with arms out stretch and a cheerful countenance.
Love is lifting with faith and hope in our Lord and Savior.
Love relies upon the calming influence of the Holy Ghost.
The psalmist said, "Trust in the Lord with all thine heart, and lean not unto
thine own understanding, and He will direct thy paths. The path is marked by
love." President Dallin H. Oaks has counseled, "Let us avoid what is harsh and
hateful. Let us seek to be holy like our Savior." That holiness comes
when we are peacemakers, when we are charitable, patient, forgiving and kind, love is expressed
in prayer, in the Book of Mormon.
