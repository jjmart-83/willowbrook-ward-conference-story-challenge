My friends, there is a sculpting taking place within each of our souls to invite the Savior
to become involved as hard.
Our pride gets in the way.
He'll lovingly chasten us to get our attention, but waits for an invitation before he can
really go to work.
Then he will start carving, sometimes surprising us with how abrupt and aggressive the work
feels.
But as time goes on, we'll begin to see the work of his hand.
God is shaping us into something only he knows we can become.
one day when all of the suffering and loneliness have ended and our pain is gone, we will be able
to step back and look into the mirror of our own soul, then we will stand all amazed and feel to
follow the Savior's feet and thank Him. We will thank Him for all of it, the molding, the shaping,
the pain and adversity that have helped us become more like Him.
