"And a little child shall lead them."
Some years ago, this scripture truly came alive to me.
It happened during a ward primary program.
That Sabbath morning, my young friend James was wearing a new suit.
As the program began, James was examining some of the more interesting aspects of his
new suit.
What most captured his attention were two features of his jacket.
On his jacket's sleeves, there were buttons but no holes.
And on the left lapel of his jacket, there was a buttonhole but no buttons.
Neither the buttons nor the buttonhole seemed to be serving any real purpose.
Well, James cleverly devised the purpose for both.
I anxiously watched as James delightedly buttoned his right sleeve to his left jacket
lapel.
Being pleased with himself quickly gave way to being panicked, because once James had
buttoned his arm to his chest, even with the flexibility of a primary child, he could not
get himself loose.
had unintentionally but quite successfully created a one-arm straight jacket.
As James became more and more stuck, I became more and more aggravated.
Baffling buttons had been put to an uncomfortable and to me irritating purpose.
I was shaking my head in disappointment as James was about to go to the pulpit.
Since then, I noticed the little girl sitting next to James.
She also had witnessed James predicament and his growing concern.
But instead of being aggravated, she wanted to help.
Quickly discerning what action was needed, she reached up with her tiny, non-judgmental
fingers and popped the button out of the buttonhole.
was rescued and the primary program was saved.
I felt relieved in that moment, but also humbled.
I recognized the quiet rebuke of misplaced buttons and tiny fingers, reminding me that
I should be trying to be more like Jesus.
That small but majestic moment helped me embrace what Elder Patrick Kieran has described as
a radical reorientation of my soul. I don't recall the topic of the primary program that day,
but the message I received through the Holy Ghost prompted a spiritual realignment.
