When the Holy Spirit of promise witnesses and confirms the efficacy of ordinances
and covenants, they become binding in time and eternity. In his sacred role as
Holy Spirit of promise, the Holy goes witnesses to the Father that the saving
ordinances have been performed properly and that the covenants associated with
them have been kept. As we honor our covenants, the Holy Spirit of Promise seals our relationships
with efficacy, virtue, and force. They are written in the Lamb's Book of Life. In contrast,
we would not expect ordinances and covenants we do not honor in faith in action, do not
choose her desire, or perhaps even disregard her mock to be binding upon us on earth or
in heaven.
