Opposition comes to all, even the righteous.
It can stir heart-rending questions in deep emotions like Joseph Smith's cry from Liberty
Jail, "O God, where art thou?" or the grief and fear felt by Helium and stripling warriors.
Such questions and emotions are not sin or weakness.
They can create a sacred space where agency is activated and we choose whether to turn
toward Jesus Christ.
Sincere seeking means turning toward Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.
