The covenants and ordinances of the Savior's restored gospel are spiritual credentials that can open wide the heavenly windows of opportunity to receive in our life's divine purpose direction and strength.
"God promises his sons and daughters' sopernal blessings for honoring covenants, including
obedience to the laws and ordinances of the gospel of Jesus Christ.
Ongoing faithfulness is required for those credentials to be valid and for promised blessings
to be received."
The Holy Ghost is the third member of the Godhead.
