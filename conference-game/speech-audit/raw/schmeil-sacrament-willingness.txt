The Babson covenant is the first covenant we make with God.
We then make a new covenant with Him each week as we partake of the sacrament during
our Sunday worship wherein we renew promises made at a baptism and covenant to always remember
the Savior.
Jesus Christ instituted the sacrament to help us remember Him with the promise that if
If we do so, we will have His Spirit with us.
In the sacrament prayer, we have a good reminder that we do not need to be perfect to partake
of the sacrament.
We need to be willing to take upon us His name, remember Him, and keep His commandments.
And in return, we have the promise to always have His Spirit with us.
The sacrament is essential to have the spirit and the strength to continue on the covenant
path.
Elder Deo Jirellen has reminded and warned us.
