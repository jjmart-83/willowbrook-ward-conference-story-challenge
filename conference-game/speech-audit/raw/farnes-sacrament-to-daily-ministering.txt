follow Him. Each Sabbath day, when you and I partake of the sacrament, we witness to our
Father in heaven our willingness to take upon ourselves the name of His Son, Jesus Christ.
That means we choose to see as He sees, feel as He feels, and do as He would do. His life
should be reflected in ours. It means that when someone encounters us, they should experience
something of Jesus Christ, His kindness, His patience, His hope, His love, His
compassion. Our promise of willingness is met by the Lord's promise that we can
always have His Spirit with us. With that gift we can minister as the Savior would
with His power and His loving kindness. We make the covenant during sacred time
with God every Sunday.
A way we keep the covenant is by ministering
to God's children every day.
Often this happens when God places someone in our pocket.
