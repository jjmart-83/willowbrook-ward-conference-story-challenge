Brothers and sisters, if you are wondering whether you are good enough to teach the
gospel of Jesus Christ, the answer is yes. Not because you know everything, not
because you have every teaching skill, not because you always feel capable and
confident, but because you can love the master teacher. And as you deepen your
love for the one you teach about, he will teach you, walk beside you, and
and gradually make you into the teacher he knows you can become.
Whether you see yourself as a scholar, an athlete, a lifelong disciple, or someone who has recently
started or started again to follow Christ, the invitation is the same. Come unto the master teacher,
learn of him, love him, and as you do, he will help you love those you teach,
and that love will make your teaching more like his.
To teach like the Savior, we must love like the Savior.
