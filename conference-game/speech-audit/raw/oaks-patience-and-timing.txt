We are all a work in progress.
We are all at different places on what President Nelson called the covenant path.
We need to be patient with one another and occasionally, even with ourselves.
Overcoming uncertainty and doubt, which may include resolving conflicts between the evolving
understanding of science and the sometimes incomplete teachings of religion can be a lengthy process.
It involves building faith and strengthening humility. Hold on to the first article of faith,
even if something else is uncertain. And while you wait, wait upon the Lord,
which includes trusting in his timing as well as in his promises.
