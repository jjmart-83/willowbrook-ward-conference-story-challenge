My dear brothers and sisters, after my wife and I completed our service as mission leaders
in the Philippine Santi Poly mission, our youngest daughter made a special request.
She asked to complete her 11th grade through a homeschool program.
My wife found an excellent school, asked me if I would stop by the Registrar's Office
and submit her remaining enrollment documents.
When arriving, I handed the paperwork to the registrar.
She passed a document back to me, pointed to a line at the bottom and asked me to write
my daughter's name and sign above it.
However, my mind was preoccupied.
I mistakenly thought she had instructed me to write my name.
With full confidence I wrote, James Fontone and signed.
A few seconds later, I realized my error.
Well, I thought, no big deal, this is an easy fix.
You see, our youngest daughter's name is Amanda James Fantoni.
So I simply added Amanda right before my own name,
and the error was quickly fixed.
