Sister Douglas and I served as mission leaders in Lima, Peru.
One of our amazing missionaries was Elder Merlin Sanchez.
Merlin was baptized when he was nearly 24 years old.
As some do, he stopped participating for a time.
A faithful friend helped him begin to fill out his mission papers and schedule a physical exam,
even though Merlin had not yet decided to serve a mission.
With the help of friends and leaders,
Merlin returned to church at age 25.
A desire to serve a mission suddenly swelled in his heart.
Thankfully, his mission papers were well advanced.
Once he made the decision to go,
it was miraculous to see how the Lord removed
seemingly insurmountable obstacles one by one.
Asking him why he wanted to serve,
He replied, "I only want to be close to Jesus Christ and to follow Him."
He became a wonderful missionary, converted to Jesus Christ in His doctrine.
His every experience, trial and companion was consecrated for His gain.
