Today, I am pleading with all of us to live life on a higher plane.
God has granted life and agency to each of us, and each of us is accountable to Him for
how we live that life.
A key part of a life well lived is contributing to the well-being of others.
It's being productive and worthy endeavors.
It's being and doing better without others doing worse.
He comes by supporting and rewarding work rather than idle pursuits.
Work leads to prosperous, just, and equitable societies.
Gambling, even for fun, leads in the opposite direction.
Remember President Oake's comments about opportunity cost, and the opportunities lost
when a person devotes time and money to gambling.
My plea again is not only to refrain from gambling, but to use the time and resources
saved thereby for doing good.
The Lord counsels us to be anxiously engaged in a good cause and bring to pass much righteousness.
Indeed, blessed are all they who do hunger and thirst.
