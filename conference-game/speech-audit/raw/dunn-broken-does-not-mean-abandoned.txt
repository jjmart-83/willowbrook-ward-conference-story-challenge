Well, to those who are nearing their mile 90,
ferrying the unimaginable,
or if you feel like I did, both depleted and defeated,
I promise that as you expand
even your last drop of energy to ask,
his helping hand will ultimately give you
that much needed leg up.
And even when the outcome or the timing
is not what you pled for, take solace,
knowing that feeling broken does not mean
you've been abandoned by God.
Or as President Dieter F. Uchtdorf taught,
often the deep valleys of our present
will be understood only by looking back on them
from the mountains of our future experience.
Often we can't see the Lord's hand in our lives
until long after the trials have passed.
Well, through him who is mighty to save,
you will find strength beyond your own.
In other words, don't confuse the perceived invisibility
of the Lord's hand with the inveterate nature
of the Lord's heart.
Peace will come even when hope for outcomes do not.
