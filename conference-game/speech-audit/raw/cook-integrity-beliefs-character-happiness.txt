We need to live our lives with integrity and follow His example.
The Lord speaking of one servant in Nauvoo declared, "He may be trusted because of the
integrity of His heart."
Priest my gospel teaches, "Integrity includes being honest with God, yourself, your leaders,
and others."
And Spencer W. Kimble taught that integrity, the willingness and ability to live by our
beliefs and commitments is one of the foundation stones of good character.
And without good character, one cannot hope to enjoy the presence of God here or in the
Eternities.
In the Father's plan, integrity, virtue, and happiness are intrinsically linked.
Living a virtuous life with integrity aligns us with God's will.
True happiness, often translated as blessedness, is the actual outcome of integrity and being
virtuous.
