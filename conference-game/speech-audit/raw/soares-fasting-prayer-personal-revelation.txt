President Joseph F. Smith thought that the proper fasting would place the body in
subjection to the spirit and so promote communion with the Holy Ghost and that
this law would bring us nearer to God. A sincere and faithful fast is accompanied
by earnest and intentional prayer. As we lift our hearts to the Lord in
humility, fasting becomes an expression of our desire to draw near to Him, communicate
the true desires of our hearts, and understand His will for us.
Through the union of fasting and prayer, our faith, love, and devotion to Him deepen.
We become more humble and more aware of our complete dependence upon the Lord.
Our hearts are softened, our souls become more receptive to the influence of the Holy Ghost.
And we are better prepared to receive personal revelation and move forward along the covenant
path.
