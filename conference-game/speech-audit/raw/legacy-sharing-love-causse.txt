question. What does the Lord expect me to do with this gift? Our testimony is not
a talent to be hidden. It is a gift to be shared with grateful hearts and in the
spirit of humility and love. Testimony and love are deeply connected gifts that
echo and strengthen one another. To say "I know" is a tender expression of love
toward our neighbor. Because we love others, we naturally desire to share with them the most
precious gift we have received, our witness of Jesus Christ and His gospel.
