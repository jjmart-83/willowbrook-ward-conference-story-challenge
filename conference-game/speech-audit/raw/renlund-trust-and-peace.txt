How often the Lord reassured the children of Israel and reassures us of His constancy,
declaring, "I will never break my covenant with you."
Perhaps you've been served more than your share of cold oatmeal.
Perhaps like the children of Israel, you've doubted God's care for you, His willingness
to sucker you, or His capacity to keep His promises.
God invites us to trust Him and His promises. During His mortal ministry, the Savior told
His disciples, "These things I've spoken to you, that in me, He might have peace. In
the world, ye shall have tribulation, but be of good cheer. I've overcome the world."
Jesus overcame the world. We don't have to do so ourselves. When our faith is centered
Jesus Christ, He promises spiritual peace no matter our circumstances. We can
experience joy in our here and now because of Him and through our covenant
relationship with Him. Faith in Jesus Christ frees us from uncertainty.
