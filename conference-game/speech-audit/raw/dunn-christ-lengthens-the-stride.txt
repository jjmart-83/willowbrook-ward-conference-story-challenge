Well, marveling at his benevolence, my thoughts immediately turn to an infinitely more amazing
load-bearing friend, our Savior, Jesus Christ, of whom Isaiah unequivocally assured has borne
our griefs and carried our sorrows, which I had always assumed meant that the trails
we travel would somehow become easier.
But as I've come to learn through this race called life and the ups and downs of it, he
simultaneously lifts and uplifts, sometimes in unseen fashion, multiplying and magnifying
even our most minuscule offerings.
In other words, the Redeemer rescues not by shortening our course, but by helping us
lengthen our stride.
Any time we turn to Christ, we turn a corner.
By his grace, we can trust and expect in his constant care.
And because God knows our prayers even before we ask him.
