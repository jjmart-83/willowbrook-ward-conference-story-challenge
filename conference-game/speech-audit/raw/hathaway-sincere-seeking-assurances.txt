Sincere seeking means turning toward Heavenly Father in Jesus Christ through heartfelt prayer,
purposeful fasting, and scripture study.
Alma's people, in bondage, began to cry mightily to God.
King Darius fasted through the night as he waited for Daniel's deliverance from the lion's den.
Such acts of faith draw us closer to Jesus Christ.
As we humbly and patiently keep our covenants, the Savior will visit us in our afflictions
and bring assurances of deliverance.
Many in the Scriptures received assurances of peace, faith, and hope for deliverance in
Jesus Christ.
Sister Kenzie Brinkerhoff was visited in her afflictions.
