When we genuinely take upon ourselves the name of Jesus Christ, by loosing ourselves
in the service of others, something miraculous happens, we experience what Alma the young
referred to as a mighty change in our hearts, the kind of change that leads to true conversion
and not merely personal elation.
King Benjamin asked this penetrating question, "For how knoweth a man the master whom he
He has not served, and who is a stranger unto Him and is far from the thoughts and intents
of His heart.
My beloved brothers and sisters, the Savior's invitation is not for us to merely add His
name to our names.
His invitation is for us to take upon us His name by covenant and to serve Him with all
of our heart might, mind, and strength by lifting, loving, and serving our fellow men.
I stand before you today as the witness of His living reality.
