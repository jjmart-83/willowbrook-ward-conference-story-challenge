One of our former missionaries wrote me recently and said,
"As for my faith, my testimony is not as certain as it once was."
To him, and to all who feel that same egg I invite you,
please come unto Christ and embrace His promise
that his joy might remain in you
and that your joy might be full.
My humble invitation to us all will you find one person
and invite him over here to come unto Christ
by exercising faith to follow his teachings
or invite a friend to meet with the missionaries
as you lose yourself in helping him or he come.
