So our family experienced a sudden trial in the Lord's
deliverance as we drove in two cars
to begin our service as mission leaders.
While driving in a construction zone,
two large tires came loose from a semi-truck
and struck both cars at high speed.
The airbags deployed, the cars were destroyed,
and we were left stranded on the roadside
wondering why this had happened when we had left everything
to serve the Lord.
In that moment, the Spirit brought us peace.
Everything would be all right.
We were not immediately delivered from the trial,
but that assurance gave us hope in the deliverance
that would come.
With the help of many wonderful people,
we arrived on time and began our mission
without serious injury.
The experience on that freeway illustrates
a scriptural pattern of divine deliverance.
