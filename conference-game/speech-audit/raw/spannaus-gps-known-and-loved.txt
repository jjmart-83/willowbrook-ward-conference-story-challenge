I want to illustrate this concept with an experience.
On my way to the dentist, I put the address in my phone.
While I was driving on the freeway,
the GPS thought I was on a parallel street
and kept telling me to turn right
when there was no place to turn.
Finally, I pull off the freeway
and call my husband for help.
When I gave him my exact location,
he was able to guide me to the place I wanted to go.
Dear young women, the Lord knows where you are.
He has your exact location and can guide you.
He knows your dreams and your gifts.
He knows when you feel alone and what your questions are.
He knows your challenges and how to turn your weaknesses
into strengths.
He knows everything and knowing everything about you does not make him love you less.
Once a young man came running kneeling before Jesus.
