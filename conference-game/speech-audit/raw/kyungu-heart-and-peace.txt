As we follow Jesus Christ, we will not only fill his peace,
but as the Alde Ulysses Chrada said,
we can become an instrument of his peace in the world.
Let us repent for the harsh language and the expression
that have offended our friends, leaders,
or family members, and colleagues.
Let us pray for peaceful communication among us,
regardless of the difference in our opinions.
Let us use the language that uplifts, edifies,
comfort, and brings people together.
Let us speak with one respect,
When we speak with each other, let us qualify for its blessings
by using the language of peacemakers.
The Savior promised, "Peace I live on with you."
