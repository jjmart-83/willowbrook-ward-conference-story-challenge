It's interesting to note that when the church was only about one and a half years old, some early church leaders opposed the idea of publishing the Lord's revelations through the Prophet Joseph that would eventually become the Doctrine and Covenants.
Some of them were embarrassed by the young prophet's poor writing skills.
Maybe that's why the Savior declared, "What I, the Lord, have spoken, I have spoken, and I excuse not myself."
Though the heavens and the earth shall pass away, my words shall not pass away, but shall
all be fulfilled, whether by my own voice or the voice of my servants, it is the same.
How grateful I am for noble progenitors.
