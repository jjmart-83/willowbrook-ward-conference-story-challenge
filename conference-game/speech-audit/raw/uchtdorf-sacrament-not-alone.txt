My dear brothers and sisters, the segment can become a fresh starting point for our personal
relationship with God and for a greater Christ-centered unity in our lives.
Have you ever noticed that the segment is the only ordinance we receive during which
we are not addressed by name, rather it is performed for the souls of all those who partake of it.
The only names that are used are those of God the Father and Jesus Christ. We partake individually,
but also as a group. There's something powerful, something empowering about renewing covenants
with God, while surrounded by brothers and sisters who have made or are considering making
the same covenants. The segment is a weekly reminder, a sacred reminder, that we are not alone.
We have the Savior, and we have each other. Wherever you are, I invite you to join the Christ
