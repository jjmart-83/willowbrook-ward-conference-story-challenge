A second way to stand for truth with love is to first find common ground.
A stake president once brought me a long list of ministering visits.
I suggested he choose just one home given our schedule limitations.
Technically, he honored the request.
But when we arrived at the home, there were five separate individuals waiting inside,
each with a completely different concern.
I asked, "Well, where do you want to start?"
As I listened to their stories, I was tempted at times to interject, but the spirit kept
prompting me to wait.
After nearly two hours of listening, going right through our next meeting, I began to
better understand their struggles, and I could feel their heartfelt strivings, and when I
invited them to come to stake conference that evening, each one of them accepted, resisting
the impulse to initially respond, built common ground, which allowed me later to teach from
a place of love.
As with the Savior, we love Him because He loved us first.
The third principle is to be a light.
