Our Savior Jesus Christ suffered for us.
He knows exactly how we feel, especially in our darkest and most trying times.
As you humbly turn to the Lord and cast your burdens on Him, you have His promise.
Draw near unto me, and I will draw near unto you.
He will provide comfort.
He will provide answers on His timetable as you turn to Him.
1. Answers will come by various means as guided by the Spirit, sometimes in the form
of tools, resources, and help from other people. The Lord is promised, and I will pray the
Father, and He shall give you another comforter.
