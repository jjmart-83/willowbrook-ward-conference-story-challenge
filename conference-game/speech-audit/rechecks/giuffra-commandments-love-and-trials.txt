A principle that has become very meaningful in our family.
We keep the commandments because we love God,
not merely because we hope to receive the blessings that come from obedience.
Commandments are given to draw us closer to the Savior,
to protect us, and to help us remain free from sin and many of the challenges the world presents.
This does not mean that we will be free from trials or difficulties.
All of us will face moments of uncertainty, financial hardship, illness, or loss.
Yet even when we do not understand the purpose of our trials, we can have the assurance that
the Lord knows our circumstances, honors our obedience, and fulfills His promises in His
own time and way.
