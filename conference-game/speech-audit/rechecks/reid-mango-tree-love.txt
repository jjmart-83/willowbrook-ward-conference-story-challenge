many of my earliest primary classes
were held beneath the shade of a mango tree
our teachers faithfully
prepared each lesson
but what I remember most
is how they loved us
they knew us personally
even by name
their love helped us feel the Savior's love
and feeling His love
helped us feel the truth of His gospel
and looking back
I realized that my parents
and my church teachers
were following the pattern of the master teacher
because the impact of a gospel teacher
is not just the message.
It is the love of the messenger.
My wife and I will always cherish our time
in the Australia City.
