The Savior's invitation is not merely for us
to add His name to our name
like a badge of religious affiliation.
The Savior whined,
Some draw near to me with their lips,
but their hearts are far from me.
His invitation for us is to take upon us His name.
It is an invitation to become more like Him
through covenant keeping.
The resurrected Lord asked,
Have they not read the scriptures, which say ye must take upon you the name of Christ, which is my name?
How do we, as covenant children of God, truly take upon ourselves the name of Jesus Christ?
How does that manifest in our daily lives?
I love the simple yet profound answer taught by the prophet Moroni.
He recorded that none were baptized, save they took upon them the name of Christ,
having a determination to serve him to the end.
Taking upon us his name requires a lifelong determination to serve him,
and King Benjamin provided the divine key.
When ye are in the service of your fellow beings, ye are only in the service of your God.
