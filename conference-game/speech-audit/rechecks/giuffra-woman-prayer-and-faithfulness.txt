When we arrived at her home and began reviewing the lessons, we came once again to allow tithing.
She said to us, "Last night I knelt in prayer and told our Heavenly Father that I truly
wanted to become a member of His Church and that He would need to help me live this law.
As we heard those words, a profound feeling of peace and assurance filled our minds and
hearts.
We knew that the Lord had heard both her prayers and ours.
She was baptized the following Saturday.
A few weeks later, I was transferred to another area, but I remained in contact with her bishop.
He told me she arrived every week with her tithing envelope, and that on the first Sunday
of every month, she also brought her fast offering.
Throughout my life, I have learned to trust in the Lord.
