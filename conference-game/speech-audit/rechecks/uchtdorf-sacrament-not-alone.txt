My dear brothers and sisters, the sacrament can become a fresh starting point for our
personal relationship with God and for a greater Christ-centered unity in our lives.
Have you ever noticed that the sacrament is the only ordinance we receive during which
we are not addressed by name?
Rather, it is performed for the souls of all those who partake of it.
The only names that are used are those of God the Father and Jesus Christ.
We partake individually, but also as a group.
There is something powerful, something empowering, about renewing covenants with God while surrounded
by brothers and sisters who have made or are considering making the same covenants.
The segment is a weekly reminder—a sacred reminder—that we are not alone.
We have the Savior, and we have each other.
