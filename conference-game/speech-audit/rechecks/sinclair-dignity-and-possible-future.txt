The great Redeemer of all knows absolutely everything about this unnamed woman,
and he comes to her to be a living witness of living water.
He offers her grace and transformative help.
He spoke to the woman with dignity, not shame.
He earnestly listened to her questions and responded with teaching, not ridicule.
He did not hold her hostage to her history.
He trusted her with truth.
Jesus Christ reminds us that to help people transform their lives,
we must look not only to their past, we must look to their possible.
She walked to the well that day as a pariah.
She walked away from the well with a purpose.
That is precisely what quick to help and slow to judge looks like.
