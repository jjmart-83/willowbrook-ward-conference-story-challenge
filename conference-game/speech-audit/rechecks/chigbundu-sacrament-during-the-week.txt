sacrament. The sacrament
is a sacred ordinance
that strengthens our relationship with the
Savior. During the sacrament
we make promises
to take upon us the name of
Jesus Christ, to always
remember him and to keep
his commandment. As we
partake of sacrament, we
can ask ourselves
what will he have me do?
As we remember Jesus Christ
throughout the week and strive
to keep his commandments, our lives are gradually refined and reshaped. Our why me questions will be
