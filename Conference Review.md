# Conference review and game foundation

**Reviewed 2026-10-05:** all **37 downloaded addresses by 36 speakers** were reread in full from their English captions and checked against the discussion notes, story catalog, and 30 proposed clues. This guide is organized by assignment and conference session.

[Project overview](README.md) · [Activity plan](Conference%20Activity%20Plan.md) · [Story catalog](Conference%20Story%20Catalog.md) · [Play the prepared clips](conference-game/clip-library.html)

## How to use this foundation

Start with the six assignment guides to balance the game. Use the session entries to compare each talk's actual teaching, strongest connections, qualifications, and prepared excerpts. The story catalog supplies illustration prompts and alternative stories; the activity plan supplies the clue questions and answer criteria.

**Direct** means the speaker explicitly teaches that assignment. **Supporting** means the passage helps a connection or application but does not fully answer the assigned question. An omitted assignment has no strong connection identified in this review.

Summaries and host answers are paraphrases. The supplied SRTs have recognition errors and overlapping cue times; their paired copies are identical. Clip selections have been checked against the complete captions and their media streams/durations are checked during extraction. Exact spoken wording and a final listen to each edit remain separate checks; caption review is not an audio audit.

### Follow Christ — Deacons

**Assigned question:** What blessings or results can we expect by following Him every day?

Daily discipleship changes choices, character, closeness to Christ, and willingness to help. Obedience expresses love and trust; the talks do not promise a particular promotion, wealth, popularity, or an easy life. Build questions around a concrete choice and the result that can reasonably follow even when circumstances remain hard.

**Question to build from:** What could following Jesus change in a decision at school or home, and what result could you hope for without assuming the problem disappears?

**Keep this qualification:** Distinguish spiritual help and growth from a guaranteed outcome or timetable. Preserve the actual subject of a tithing or integrity passage instead of turning it into a universal reward formula.

### Know Your Worth — Builders of Faith

**Assigned question:** How does knowing who we are change how we see ourselves and treat others?

Divine identity is deeper than achievement, social labels, or a mistake. The practical test is how that belief changes the way we see ourselves and recognize, include, and help others. Pair the teaching of enduring love with a story showing respectful treatment; identity should lead to both reassurance and constructive growth.

**Question to build from:** How would you respond to a friend calling themself worthless after a mistake, and how would that same teaching change the way you treat a classmate?

**Keep this qualification:** Enduring worth does not make choices inconsequential. Do not presume every participant has experienced a loving home. Preserve Spannaus's address to young women when using exact wording.

### Find Guidance — Teachers

**Assigned question:** How can we seek, recognize, and act on guidance from the Spirit?

The strongest examples show preparation, recognition, and a response: prayer with fasting or scripture study; an enlightening thought, prompting, warning, comfort, or testimony; then a faithful action. Gilbert's instruction to wait is a useful reminder that responding can mean restraint and careful listening. Use several sources if one excerpt does not demonstrate all three steps.

**Question to build from:** Use one story detail for seeking, recognizing, and acting, then propose a realistic response in a disagreement or opportunity to help.

**Keep this qualification:** A pleasant feeling alone is not a universal test of revelation. Prophetic teaching, the Holy Spirit of Promise, and ordinary personal guidance are related but distinct. A talk about teaching a lesson does not automatically answer this assignment.

### Find Strength — Messengers of Hope

**Assigned question:** What can we do when life is hard or our faith is strained?

Christ can give peace, companionship, practical help through others, and strength to continue while a challenge remains. Hathaway, Runia, and Dunn offer memorable illustrations; Oaks directly addresses questions and strained faith. Ask what faithful progress or compassionate support can look like without demanding an immediate resolution.

**Question to build from:** What emotional support and practical help could you offer someone who has prayed and tried but is still struggling?

**Keep this qualification:** Do not equate pain, doubt, or delayed relief with weak faith. A shaping analogy does not establish that every harmful act was caused or approved by God. Avoid making tragic details into trivia or promising a quick cure.

### Keep Promises — Priests

**Assigned question:** How do our covenants connect us to Jesus Christ and help us become more like Him?

Covenants describe a continuing relationship with Christ, expressed in willingness, remembrance, obedience, worship, and service. The sacrament connects those commitments with the promised companionship of the Spirit. Temple stories show practical preparation and support; baptism and shared discipleship show belonging and bearing burdens.

**Question to build from:** Choose one sacrament promise and a specific way to live it on Monday. What can a youth do now to prepare for temple worship?

**Keep this qualification:** Keep Bednar's explicit limit: hardship or injury through no fault of our own does not invalidate covenants. Covenant influence does not remove another person's agency. Do not test personal worthiness or private temple details.

<a id="topic-serving"></a>

### Help Others — Gatherers of Light

**Assigned question:** How can we help others feel the Savior's love?

Seeing a person, learning their name, listening honestly, and giving practical help make love concrete. Farnes, Chibota, Sinclair, and Reid supply ordinary examples. Rasband preserves love and personal choice; Gilbert connects conviction with patience and charity. Invitations should grow out of care rather than making friendship conditional on a response.

**Question to build from:** Who might be overlooked in an ordinary day, and what specific action would help them feel known and cared for while respecting their choice?

**Keep this qualification:** Do not pressure participation, override agency, or treat winning an argument as successful ministry. Suggested present-day actions should be identified as applications when the source story does not report those exact steps.

### D. Todd Christofferson

[Original video](talks/D%20Todd%20Christofferson%20%C2%A6%20October%202026%20General%20Conference/D.%20Todd%20Christofferson%20%C2%A6%20October%202026%20General%20Conference%20%281080p_30fps_H264-128kbit_AAC%29.mp4) · [Captions](talks/D%20Todd%20Christofferson%20%C2%A6%20October%202026%20General%20Conference/D.%20Todd%20Christofferson%20%C2%A6%20October%202026%20General%20Conference%20%281080p_30fps_H264-128kbit_AAC%29.English.srt) · [Speaker screenshot](conference-game/assets/speakers/d-todd-christofferson.jpg)

Christofferson explicitly warns against gambling, emphasizing how continuous mobile access can displace savings, studies, relationships, and worthwhile work. He combines opportunity cost and the influence of one's example with a moral argument about taking rather than giving and responsible stewardship. His concluding invitation is to use the saved time and resources for good; the gambling subject should remain visible when applying the principles more broadly.

## Review findings

- All 37 addresses were reread from 7,213 caption cues and compared with all 133 catalog entries and all 30 selected board stories. The clue answers remain supported; the playable Conference Story Challenge now implements the browser board.
- The six assignments are assessed separately for every talk. Direct means the speaker explicitly teaches the assignment; supporting means the material helps an application without supplying the full answer. Teaching a lesson does not by itself establish a connection to personal revelation.
- A prayer or quiet reflection in Andersen's courage-and-faith talk is a supporting connection for Find Guidance. Soares, Kearon, Lebethoa, Chibota, and Gilbert provide clearer examples of seeking, recognizing, or responding to spiritual guidance.
- Most catalog corrections extend source windows so the application, outcome, or limiting sentence remains available. Stable story IDs, image prompts, and all 30 proposed clue answer criteria have been preserved.
- The 100-value peaches clue now asks about one childhood farm stand; the caption does not identify it as the family's favorite. Its answer remains peaches.
- Kyungu recommends a language-replacement exercise; the opening is not a report of a completed personal change. Reid specifically describes his mother as having limited formal education; that detail should not be attributed to both parents.
- Villanueva's disappointed-promotion account specifically concerns tithing and a hoped-for salary increase. No later employment outcome is reported. A broader youth application should be clearly identified as an application.
- At Bethesda, the 38 years describes the man's infirmity, not a documented length of time waiting at the pool. Sikahema introduces the proposed reason for an early revelation declaration tentatively; preserve that uncertainty.
- Bednar's passport illustration has separate excerpts for the story, the adversity qualification, and the authorized-stamp explanation. The comparison must not suggest that injury or hardship through no fault of our own invalidates covenants.
- The new library contains story and teaching excerpts from every address, including non-story counsel in Oaks's closing message. The earlier 20 speaker-identification clips remain available to the prototype.
- All 37 speaker name/title images were checked for readable text and correct address association. The two Oaks sources remain separate, and Fale's verified 00:10 image override is retained.
- Session membership was checked against the four official October 2026 session summaries. The review and catalog now group addresses by session and alphabetize within each session. Download-batch history is kept in the archive.
- Content and boundaries were reviewed using the supplied captions. Extracted files receive audio/video stream and duration checks; exact spoken quotations and final edit listening remain unverified.
