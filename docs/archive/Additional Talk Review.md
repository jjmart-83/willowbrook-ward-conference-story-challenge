## President Oaks's closing/temple address

**Inventory ID:** `dallin-h-oaks-temples`. His earlier address remains `dallin-h-oaks`; each now has its own video/caption references and prepared still.

There is useful teaching beyond temple news:

- **00:00–00:32:** center worship, learning, and testimony on Jesus Christ and the Father's plan.
- **00:32–00:56:** study conference messages, ponder them prayerfully, and act in faith on their principles. He includes the Lord's promise of further receiving. This is a direct **Deacons** connection and an excellent brief invitation for the activity's closing.
- **02:05–02:30:** a blessing of peace and contentment and of service in challenging times. Supporting **Hope/Gatherers** material, without an extended explanation of how to cope or serve.
- **02:30–03:05:** testimony about Christ's restored gospel and the covenant path toward the destiny Heavenly Father desires. Supporting **Priests** material, although Schmeil, Gong, and the other covenant passages explain the assignment more fully.

The central section welcomes public participation in the Salt Lake Temple celebration and related video/virtual-tour resources. It is relevant as an invitation to learn about the temple, but does not itself provide much instruction about sacrament promises or preparation for ordinances. No list of newly announced temple locations appears in the supplied captions.

**Recommendation:** use **00:32–00:56** to connect the game's private next step with President Oaks's invitation. It need not become another scored round. Keep the other Oaks address for the fuller discussion of questions, doubts, and daily faith. The captions garble the historical opening reference; this review does not reproduce or verify announcement dates or historical figures.

## Andrea Muñoz Spannaus

**Direct:** Builders, Teachers, Hope, Deacons, and Priests through sacrament/baptismal covenants. **Supporting:** Gatherers through real relationships and Christ's loving approach.

Her address speaks directly to young women, making it a useful addition alongside the other sources:

- **01:52–02:18:** the Lord knows their situation, dreams, gifts, questions, and weaknesses; knowing them fully does not lessen His love.
- **02:40–03:36:** Christ's loving look at the rich young man precedes His invitation to improve. God's love includes patience and opportunities to learn.
- **03:35–04:09:** beloved daughters of Heavenly Parents, divine nature and destiny, individual work, and joy through love and trust. Strong **Builders** foundation.
- **04:08–04:37:** listen to the Spirit to choose a focus, partake of the sacrament humbly, remember covenant promises, and ask daily for the Spirit's companionship. Direct **Teachers/Priests** connection.
- **04:33–05:53:** daily repentance, scripture study, and other habits and relationships that support spiritual growth. Use this as a choice of one feasible practice rather than a quiz about memorizing seven items.
- **05:53–07:18:** inadequacy while teaching in a new language and reassurance through a priesthood blessing.
- **07:14–07:45:** the Lord's greater power, guidance, baptismal covenants, and assurance of divine help. Strong **Hope** material.

**Use:** pair the divine-identity passage with Chibota or Fale to discuss how being known and loved changes self-perception and treatment of others. Her GPS story illustrates God's knowledge and guidance; it is not a promise of instant directions for every decision. Keep the speaker's address to young women intact when presenting a quotation.

## Clark G. Gilbert

**Direct:** Gatherers and Deacons; Teachers through responding to promptings while listening. **Supporting:** Hope through compassion for a struggling friend, and Priests through covenant example.

- **00:00–01:40:** standing for truth with love and examining our own hearts rather than only identifying others who need correction.
- **02:30–03:45:** avoid escalating contentious settings; sometimes a dignified pause is the right response.
- **04:28–05:26:** an angry reply may win a debate while losing the Spirit and failing to help a friend. Useful for a real youth disagreement scenario.
- **06:00–06:48:** the Spirit prompts him to wait instead of interrupting. Listening helps him understand people's struggles and later teach with love. Direct **Teachers** example with a **Gatherers** application.
- **06:47–08:02:** be a light through lived faith and covenant example.
- **08:00–10:40:** maintain convictions while reaching out in love; do not selectively keep just one part of that teaching.
- **11:15–11:48:** truth and love walk together; some need more conviction and others more charity.

**Use:** repair a fictional response to disagreement. A good answer can explain a belief calmly, ask a sincere question, listen, or pause. Neither winning the argument nor abandoning a belief is the goal. Avoid making the game a debate about another participant's private choices.

## Gabriel W. Reid

**Direct:** Gatherers, Deacons, and Hope through inadequacy in serving/teaching. **Supporting:** Builders through feeling known and capable of growth.

- **00:40–02:16:** feeling inadequate; Christ's invitation includes His help to become what He needs us to be. We are not meant to do His work alone.
- **02:17–02:59:** discipleship and love precede teaching; love the Lord and those we teach.
- **04:06–04:44:** teachers know children personally and by name; their love helps the children feel the Savior's love.
- **06:22–07:18:** focus lessons on helping people connect with Christ.
- **07:15–08:20:** willingness and love matter even when knowledge, skills, or confidence feel incomplete; the Savior helps us grow.

**Use:** strengthen the Gatherers discussion of why knowing a name and caring personally matters, and the Hope discussion of doing something worthwhile while feeling inadequate. His role and the talk's teaching theme do **not** automatically make this a lead for the group named Teachers: their assigned subject is personal revelation and the Holy Ghost.

## Moisés Villanueva

**Direct:** Deacons through tithing/obedience and blessings; Hope through disappointed expectations and strained faith. **Supporting:** Teachers through recognizing blessings and the Spirit's influence.

- **00:00–01:28:** his mother's faith amid financial difficulty and the family's effort to live the gospel.
- **01:59–02:59:** blessings may differ from our preferred form or timetable; a faithful woman's expected promotion has not come. Significant blessings may be subtle and overlooked.
- **02:55–04:20:** he reflects on blessings, acknowledges the Spirit's help, and invites the woman to make her own list.
- **07:01–07:58:** God knows our needs; commandments and blessings are connected.

**Use:** ask what someone might notice or learn when the expected result does not arrive. Keep the actual tithing context, then distinguish any broader youth application. His health/employment experiences are personal examples, not guarantees that every obedient person will receive those same outcomes. Do not use the exercise to imply that hardship proves disobedience or that gratitude should silence a concern.

## Paul H. Sinclair

**Direct:** Gatherers and Deacons. **Teachers** also has an explicit personal-learning example. **Supporting:** Builders through dignity and looking beyond someone's history.

- **00:00–02:23:** a child accidentally buttons his sleeve to his jacket; Sinclair feels irritated while another child quietly helps him get free.
- **02:22–03:16:** the experience prompts humility and learning through the Holy Ghost; a disciple is quick to help and slow to judge.
- **05:23–06:12:** Christ treats the woman at the well with dignity, listens, and offers transformative help instead of ridicule.
- **06:36–07:58:** help people in small ways, be more like Christ, and offer belonging.

**Use:** one of the strongest new game candidates. Introduce the button predicament briefly, then play **02:22–03:16** or a shorter audio-checked portion. Teams decide how to move from judging a mistake to providing useful help. The adult's irritation is part of his self-correction; do not turn the child's difficulty into a ridicule challenge or require youth to recreate the restraint.

## Thabo Lebethoa

**Direct:** Teachers, Hope, and Deacons. **Supporting:** Gatherers through peaceful communication and repentance in relationships.

- **00:30–02:16:** an argument prompts reflection; Christ's words about contention enlighten his mind and help him know what to do.
- **02:25–03:37:** Christ gives light and hope amid daily difficulties, questions, fear, and inadequacy.
- **03:34–05:05:** trust in Christ and build faith through intentional, daily prayer and scripture study.
- **05:01–05:54:** sincere, consistent scripture study matters more than imposing a single method or schedule.
- **05:53–06:28:** a person learns from scripture and acts on enlightenment from the Spirit. His coffee example illustrates change; youth can discuss another appropriate constructive action.
- **06:25–07:58:** daily repentance, changes in time/media/language, and the Savior's help.

**Use:** the most useful new addition for Teachers' **seek → recognize → act** question. His personal opening also shows that guidance may come through remembering scripture. Frame the claim about a better tomorrow as Christ-centered hope rather than a guarantee that every external circumstance improves overnight. The caption's scripture reference appears unreliable; verify it before putting a reference on a card.

## Recommended changes to activity planning

Retain the six-topic structure. Add Sinclair as an alternative to the service mission, Gilbert as a listening/disagreement case, and Lebethoa as a scripture-to-action case. Spannaus offers an especially relevant identity/hope source addressed to young women. Oaks's short study/ponder/act passage fits the closing well. The remaining strong sources do not all need separate rounds.

The existing 20 excerpt cards remain the prototype deck. These candidate passages have been reviewed for content but have not been substituted into the game data. When choosing final clips, update their evidence questions and answer keys together.
