# Original fireside assignment sheet

**Archived October 5, 2026:** original document retained after consolidation. Use [Conference Review.md](../../Conference%20Review.md#assignments) for current material. Local links have been rebased for this archive location.

| Group | Topic to listen for | Main Discussion Question |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Deacons | Following Jesus Christ  | What blessings or results can expect by following him every day?  |
| Builders of Faith | Our Identity and worth as Children of God | How does knowing who we are change how we see ourselves and treat others? |
| Teachers | Recognizing the Holy Ghost and receiving personal Revelation  | How can we seek, recognize, and act on guidance from the Spirit |
| Messengers of Hope | Finding strength in Christ through challenges  | What can we do when life is hard or our faith is strained? |
| Priests  | Covenants, the sacrament and temple preparation  | How do our covenants connect us to Jesus Christ and help us become more like him?  |
| Gatherers of Light  | Loving, serving, and inviting others to Christ | How can we help others feel the Savior’s love?  |
